Plane of Hate Innoruuk

Monster level 48+
Min. player level 46
Zone tags hateplane / hateplaneb
ZEM 86 (115%)

Access

  • Wizard spell Alter Plane: Hate (level 46 Wizard, 300 mana, reagent Fuligan Soulstone of Innoruuk, ~62.5pp from wizard guild vendors in Erudin, Neriak 3rd Gate, or Southern Felwithe) — group teleport.
  • One-way portal in Oasis of Marr: the black sword atop the tower leads to the public instance; the voidling leads to the raid instance.
  • No conventional zone exit — Gate/Origin/port out only.

Layout & Mechanics

Two-floor zone. Key landmarks: Port In, The Church, the Organ Hall (where Maestro of Rancor holds his raid encounter, spans both floors), and a spiral staircase near the Fountain leading to floor 2. Many mobs see through Invisibility and Invisibility vs. Undead. Innoruuk himself wanders floor 2 rather than holding a fixed spawn point. Several minibosses are simply elite ("bigger, meaner") reskins of common trash — e.g. Avatar of Abhorrence is an elite "an abhorrent."

Loot Groups (trash armor drops)

  • Group 1: Ethereal Mist (CLR), Vermiculated (DRU), Rune Etched (SHM), Anthemion (BST), Thorny Vine (RNG), Valorium (PAL), Shadow Rage (BER), Shiverback-hide (MNK), plus Lustrous Russet Armor.
  • Group 2: Insidious (ENC), Apothic (MAG), Blighted (NEC), Carmine (WIZ), Imbrued (BRD), Umbral Platemail (SHD), Woven Shadow (ROG), Indicolite (WAR), plus Midnight Clad Armor.
  • A haunted chest can drop any planar armor piece, including Plane of Fear pieces.

Quests

  • Natures Defender Quest (Paladin, Tunare deity-locked): starts at Plane of Growth from the Guardian of Takish, min. level 46, spans Plane of Hate + The Wakening Land. Requires the Fleshless Skull from Corrupter of Life (Hate) plus Corrupted Fauns/Panther/Unicorns Skin (Wakening Land). Reward: Natures Defender (2H Slashing, PAL, Nature's Holy Wrath proc).
  • The "Innoruuk Symbol Quests" line is thematically tied to Innoruuk but takes place entirely outside this zone (Neriak/Nektulos/Lesser Faydark/Steamfont) — does not require entering Plane of Hate.
  • No key/flag quest gates zone entry — access is level + spell/portal only.

Plane of Fear Cazic Thule

Monster level 48+
Min. player level 46
Zone tag fearplane
ZEM ~82.5 (110%)

Access

One-way portal in the spectre caves of The Feerrott. No key/flag item — level-gated only. Only way out is gate/port, or a temple exit portal deep in the zone (not recommended — see-invis mobs can aggro through walls/ceiling near it).

Mechanics

Extreme mob aggro ranges; most mobs see through invisibility. Killing Cazic Thule triggers zone-wide social aggro from every mob while he's engaged. Cazic Thule's kit includes a near-unresistable single-target Panic fear (frequent proc), an AE snare, and Avatar Power (100 direct damage + knockback + top-slot dispel).

Loot Groups

  • Group 1: Wisdom casters, Berserkers, Monks + Lustrous Russet Armor.
  • Group 2: Intelligence casters, Rogues, Warriors + Midnight Clad Armor.
  • Phoboplasm (Gelatinous Cube) has an expanded loot table that can include other planes' armor, including Plane of Hate pieces.

Quests

  • Enchanter Epic Quest: requires killing Wraith of a Shissir (Amygdalan Temple) for Head of the Serpent — one of four components (with Essence of a Ghost from The Hole, Essence of a Vampire from Plane of Hate, Sands of the Mystics from Field of Bone) combined into the 4th Piece of Staff toward the Staff of the Serpent.
  • Paladin Epic Quest: starts here via Irak Altil; final turn-in (Mark of Atonement + Fiery Avenger) to Irak Altil yields Fiery Defender.
  • Cazic Thule, Dread, Fright, and Terror are each tagged with "Related Quests" for the Necromancer, Shadow Knight, Wizard, Shaman, and Warrior Epic Quests, but the exact components/steps weren't confirmed this pass — flagged for follow-up research rather than published as fact.

Plane of Sky Veeshan

Monster level 50+
Min. player level 46
Adjacent zone East Freeport

Access

Orb on a rock in the East Freeport bay (~425,-1200), or Wizard spell Alter Plane: Sky (reagent: Cloudy Stone of Veeshan) — returns a group to Island 1 with keys intact. All buffs/debuffs strip on entry. No in-zone teleport except Alter Plane: Sky. Falling/jumping off an island destroys all Sky keys and dumps the player back in the East Freeport bay; death and Druid Wind of the South/North also exit.

Structure

Nine-stop island chain (8 numbered islands + Island 1.5), gated by one-way teleport pads requiring a key dropped by the previous island's boss. Keys are permanent on the keyring once used and (unlike classic EQ) drop only from bosses, never trash. Some islands are reachable by jumping between vertically-overlapping islands instead (7→2, 4→2, 4→3, 6→5). Islands 2, 5, 6 start aggressive; Island 4 turns aggressive after the first kill. Boss NPCs no longer have deathtouch. Zone is Veeshan's dominion — genies, harpies, fairie dragons, and other exotic mob types populate it, with her physical form (Eye of Veeshan) as the final boss.

Class Quests ("Plane of Sky Tests")

All 16 classes have a dedicated multi-stage "Test of [Trait]" chain, each given by a unique NPC and gated behind a randomly-dropping Wind Rune (any Sky mob) plus an island-specific quest item.

Completing a class's full chain unlocks that class as a selectable Primary Class option in loadouts. Confirmed reward examples: Cleric — Necklace of Resolution (uses Silvered Spiroc Necklace, Island 6) and Baton of the Sky (uses Mithril Bands, Island 8); Magician — Duennan Shielding Ring (uses Golden Efreeti Ring, Island 7); Shaman — Vermilion Sky Ring. Melee classes can earn a 21% haste belt mid-chain and a 41% haste belt at the end (e.g. Monk's Golden Sash of Tranquility, Rogue's Renard's Belt of Quickness).

Epic quest tie-ins: Magician, Monk, Ranger, and Paladin epics pull components from Sky. The Warrior Epic Quest specifically needs Spiroc Wingblades from The Spiroc Lord (Island 5) — the wiki notes the item's "Ranger" tag is misleading; it is not used by any Ranger quest.

Deep Itemization (confirmed to exist, not yet fully itemized)

An Efreeti weapon/item line spans War Axe, Bow, Club, Horn, Maul, Shield, Spear, Staff, Standard, Statuette, Zweihander, Battle Axe, Dirk, Great Staff, Long Sword, Mace, Magi Staff, Scimitar, and Wind Staff variants, plus a Golden Efreeti armor set (Bracers, Chestplate, Gauntlets, Greaves, Ring, Turban, Vambraces). The wiki's category page lists 325 pages tagged to this zone — most of the zone's best loot comes from the class-quest chains rather than direct boss drops.
