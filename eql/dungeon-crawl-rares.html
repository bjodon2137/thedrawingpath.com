Dungeon Crawl: Required Rares by Zone

The 9/9/26 patch replaced Non-Respawning Zones with Dungeon Crawl instances, available in indoor dungeons and all 3 planes. To finish one, you must slay every rare creature in the zone plus enough of the general population. This page compiles, zone by zone, the named/rare creatures that count — sourced from eqlwiki.com, cross-checked against EQLForge and community blogs, since this feature is only 2 days old and no source exposes the game's literal internal flag.

21 zones covered
2 zones flagged as likely invalid crawl targets
Research pass: 2026-09-11

How Dungeon Crawl actually works

Straight from eqlwiki.com/Dungeon_Crawl, quoting the game's own tooltip: nothing you kill respawns naturally; to finish, you must slay every rare creature and enough of the zone's original population; you earn motes and experience, and everything you killed may add loot/coin to a reward chest; the easier the zone is for you, the smaller the payout; finishing refunds your instance charge; and rare creatures show up on your map while a crawl is active (but only if you can see them — some are invisible).

Secrets [EQL] (dev), official Discord, 9/9/26:
"Rare named chances are not increased in Dungeon Crawls. They just appear on the map. We've mostly de-PH'd zones... the whole rare spawn chance thing doesn't really apply anymore."
"[Plane of Fear] There are not [any labeled rares]. And that's a bonus and detriment... can continue to run them over and over."
"All of the Plane of Sky mobs are also considered rare creatures now, too."
"[Groups] You need to be there for a majority of kills to qualify... There are solo and multiplayer crawls... [XP] Personalized."

The one fully-documented run: a community blogger (chasingdings.com) completed a Nagafen's Lair (Sol B) crawl needing exactly 10 named kills — Kobold Noble, Kobold Champion, Kobold Priest, Kobold King, Stone Spider, Noxious Spider, Death Beetle, Efreeti Lord Djarn, Targin the Rock, and an invisible goblin (Guano Harvester) that doesn't show on the map without See Invisible. Three other well-known Sol B named mobs — King Tranix, Warlord Skarlon, Magus Rokyl — were not required, confirming that not every proper-named NPC in a zone counts.

Read this with real caution. Nothing below carries that same first-hand certainty except Sol B (not included in the tables below since it's already fully documented in the quote above). Every other zone's list is a proxy — either eqlwiki's Notable/Rare NPC field, or a fan site's rare tag — validated only indirectly against the dev's statement that rares are each zone's pre-existing named/rare-flagged NPCs.

Plane of Fear 2 entries

CONFIRMED NO RARES — direct from the dev on Discord: “Are there any labeled rare creatures there? There are not.” Independently corroborated by the zone's own wiki page (“no rare/named mobs with placeholders”). Completion runs on the general-population kill threshold alone; you lose the bonus mote/loot roll that killing a “named” gives elsewhere, but you can re-run it repeatedly since nothing gates you on a rare spawn.
CreatureSource confidenceNotes
No rare-flagged creatures exist in this zone. Confirmed (dev/first-hand) Fixed named uniques exist (Irak Altil, Wraith of a Shissir, Phoboplasm) but none are flagged rare — they're part of the ordinary population.
Multiple common mobs see through invisibility at the entrance and throughout Wiki-confirmed Spinechiller spider, scareling, shiverback, boogeyman, phantasm, samhain, fetid fiend, Wraith of a Shissir all always see invis.
