Dungeon Crawl Guide — By Level

Every hunting spot from eqlwiki.com's own Per-Level Hunting Guide, organized into 12 level brackets from 1 through 59. Originally written as a solo-hunting guide, so treat "Good for: X" tags and free-text notes as class-suitability hints layered on a mostly-solo framework rather than a formal group/raid column.

Sourced verbatim from eqlwiki.com/Per-Level_Hunting_Guide
Effective cap: 55-59 bracket folds in level-60 "buffer" camps
Server-power note (the guide's own framing): "Characters on this server generally perform like characters three or more levels higher. As such, mobs in an area of your level should be blue, white, or yellow, give or take." Level ranges below reflect the wiki's own listed brackets, not a re-derivation.
Source note: This is a direct transcription of eqlwiki.com's community-maintained guide (its own Credits section says it was "shamelessly taken from other guides, forum posts, and even other sites" and consolidated). Named-mob timers, faction notes, and coordinates are reproduced as written — treat exact numbers as directional, and coordinates/named-mob states as subject to drift since the page was last edited.
