Beastlord Content Gap

No BIS data exists. eqlwiki.com's Beastlord "Gearing" section has the expected tier subheadings (Pre-Planar/Planar/Kunark/Velious Pre-Raid/Velious Raiding) but every underlying shared gear-reference page is unpopulated for this class — every other class checked has data there, Beastlord does not. The "Equipping a Beastlord" page is empty, and `Category:Beastlord_Equipment` is an unranked, unfiltered ~84-item catalog explicitly marked "WIP." Pet-focus items also don't exist yet in EQL. Do not backfill this from classic EQ or by analogy to Shaman/Ranger — flagged for a follow-up pass, ideally via interactive (non-static) access to eqlegendstools.com's BIS tool.

Berserker Content Gap

Unlike every other of the 16 classes, none of eqlwiki.com's five gear-reference pages (Pre-Planar/Planar/Kunark/Velious Pre-Raid/Velious Raiding) contain a Berserker section at all — confirmed by checking each page directly via the MediaWiki API. Cross-Class Notes

  • Shared caster itemization: Golden Efreeti Boots, Mystic Cloak, Djarn's Amethyst Ring, Black Sapphire Platinum Necklace, and Rokyls Channeling Crystal recur across Necromancer, Wizard, Magician, and Enchanter — these look like shared INT/WIS-caster pieces rather than class-exclusive drops, useful when gearing a multi-caster group.
  • "First item = BIS" convention: explicitly confirmed on the Paladin and Cleric source pages; extended here to the other melee/hybrid classes' Velious Raiding lists on the same assumption. The four pure-caster classes (Necromancer, Wizard, Magician, Enchanter) do not get this treatment — their pages either explicitly disclaim strict ranking or simply list unranked options.
  • Ammo slot: not itemized as BIS for any class checked, including Ranger where it's likely to matter most.
  • +10 / Exaltation upgrades: no class page ties its BIS list to a specific upgrade level. Treat everything above as base-item guidance until a follow-up pass can confirm how upgrades shift rankings.
  • Content gaps: Berserker and Beastlord have no BIS data on eqlwiki.com at all — this is the wiki's gap, not this research pass's. Monk's raid-tier gearing is only partially documented (Plane of Sky quest rewards are solid, Kunark/Velious-raid tiers are missing).
